# Show junction movement behaviour

Shows observed vehicle and pedestrian behaviour at corridor junctions, with period filters and directional charts.

## Includes

- Junction map, volume comparison, pedestrian overview, and vehicle mix
- Weekday/weekend and time-period filters

## Excludes

- Modelled walk-access catchments
- YouTube or Google Maps review mining
