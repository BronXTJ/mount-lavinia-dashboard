# Overview

Presents the Galle Road corridor at a glance: division KPIs, population structure, land-use mix, the interactive study-area map, and live weather and air quality.

## Includes

- Five Grama Niladhari divisions as the study-area frame
- Baseline demographics and land-use mix on the Overview tab
- Live Open-Meteo forecast and air-quality fields on the Overview map

## Excludes

- Focus Area network and environmental grid analyses
- Side research not on GitHub Pages (social media analysis, condo inventory, park interventions)
- The unused `/land-use` route stub, which is not in the sidebar
