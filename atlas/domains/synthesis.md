# Synthesis

Integrates spatial findings into a planning story: catalogued findings, story spine, and links into Issues and Potentials.

## Includes

- Integrated findings and cross-theme navigation
- Deep links into Focus Area subsections where the story requires them

## Excludes

- Raw GIS processing and Excel sources
- SWOT, PESTEL, and SFA scoring (those live under Issues and Potentials)
