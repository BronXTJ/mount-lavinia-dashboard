# Environmental Analysis

Modelled microclimate on a 10 m grid: thermal comfort (UTCI), urban heat, and sky view factor, with map-linked panels.

## Includes

- UTCI, UHI, and SVF layers on the Environmental Analysis tab
- Map-first stacked layout on smaller screens

## Excludes

- Live Open-Meteo fields (those belong on Overview)
- Building-height research extracts that are not this tab
